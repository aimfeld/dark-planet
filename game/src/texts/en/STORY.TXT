How It All Began...
It happened a long, long time ago...
when demons, dwarves, elves and
warlocks still walked the earth.

It all began on a little patch of
earth that eked out its existence
still untouched by the lurking shadows
of infernal evil. A peaceful old
Germanic civilization reveled in the
fruits of its diligence and
contentment. But then, one day
pregnant with doom, a fireball of
destruction came crashing down on the
blooming meadows, and King Valandir
and his loyal people believed
themselves already slain by the
double-edged sword of Salandriel, the
angel of death, and banished forever
to the catacombs of hellish wrath.

The impact swept away half the
village, and the grief and the pain
for the fallen were great. They
thought the crashed object, which went
on smoking for days, was a spawn of
the devil, created by the sins that
only blood vengeance can atone for.
But when after ten moons nothing about
'the Thing', as the twelve sages of
the Order of Ezekiel called it from
then on, had stirred, Balduron and his
men resolved to take a closer look at
the Thing. Supported by the prayers of
their people and the symbol of the
Fivefold Unity, they set out to face
the supposed trial.
In reverent awe they marveled at the
Thing, which rose before them to a
seemingly endless height. The Thing
was made mostly of a material
completely unknown to Balduron and his
troop. Today the Thing would probably
have been identified as a UFO, but
back then, in those ancient times,
nobody talked of such things yet. The
Thing's impact had torn a huge hole in
its side, through which our friends
entered the spacecraft.

All manner of mysterious devices were
inside, but Balduron the alchemist
could make no sense of them, so he
asked the grey wizard Fladnag for his
infinitely sublime and wise counsel,
blessed by the Order of Hurfalsin.
Fladnag spoke the Words of Light, and
the room was bathed in a spass, cold
light. Then followed the Words of
Synergetic Recovery, and all around
our heroes glaring lights flashed and
the ship quaked under the baleful
powers of grey magic. A hum went
through the Thing, swelling to a
roaring drone as the UFO's thrusters
started. As if hypnotized, in complete
absence of mind, Fladnag staggered
across the bridge toward the main
computer and, by an unintended
telepathic command, activated not the
self-destruct sequence (or the story
would have been rather short) but the
launch program, which began the return
to the alien home planet of the
extraterrestrial object.
Balduron and his men slowly got to
know the on-board appliances, for
example the microwave, to cook lasagna
for Garfildon the tomcat.

But in the loneliness a quarrel broke
out, for half of the pack absolutely
wanted to watch The Simpsons on the
holodeck, while the other half would
rather have indulged in daytime soaps.
Thus a wedge was driven into the unity
of the heroes and heroines, and wrath
flared up in the blazing flames of
Lucifer's fumes. The brawl degenerated
into bloody combat and desperate
rampages, and an axe blow into the
on-board computer activated the
hologram of an alien being, which
spoke to them...

nothing!!! But should anyone ever lay

A jolt went through the ship, the
ship's mighty gate opened, and full of
fighting spirit Balduron and Fladnag
stormed outside with their followers.
But they could not believe their eyes
when the embodiment of evil, a
diabolical, exorbitant, gigantic
monster with its hosts of devil's
harlots and demons, awaited them. With
eyes of fire and destruction it stared
down at them, and a glance of unholy
fierceness struck the first man to set
foot on the cursed planet, and he
burned with a scream of hellish agony.
The giant being stepped out of the
gloomy shadow of the black darkness,
and Balduron saw that it was a spawn
of fire, like an elemental that could
call undead earth and lava its main
components.

The golem-like being raised its voice,
accompanied by thunder from the fiery
heights of the sky, and spoke words of
annihilation. But Fladnag the Grey
focused his magic powers on the
elemental and blocked the attack with
a shield of pure auric energy.
The earth quaked under the scornful
laughter of the army of darkness, and
the golem spoke:
"Your ridiculous power isn't even
enough to tickle me! You cannot kill
me, for I have long been dead!". In a
roaring storm straight from hell,
stone and fire came down from the sky
and struck Fladnag's protective wall
with tremendous force.
Fladnag's eyes began to sparkle and a
twitch of madness played about his
mouth as he screamed in a trembling
voice:
"I can protect you no longer, my
companions! Flee! HAHAHAAA! FLEE, YOU
FOOLS! FOR ONLY THE PENITENT MAN WILL
PASS!
AAAAAHHHHHH!". With an incredible feat
of strength he held the shield a
little longer, but then the fiery
waves struck off the heroes' arms and
legs, and they perished in agony in
the shadow of the giant elemental that
ruled the DARK PLANET.
A tremor seized the eternal universe,
for the balance between good and evil
was broken. The reign of terror had
begun.
The unholy pack searched the spaceship
and found records of its route, and so
the planet Earth became their next
target!
A death ship of gigantic size,
equipped with everything needed to set
up another reign of terror, was sent
out to seal the fate of the Earth.
The Golem chose the most bestial
creatures to undertake the glorious
mission.
Mankind, still primitive and backward
in weapons technology, had no idea of
its coming doom.
The subspace singularity generators
were set to six hundred and sixty-six

about 1000 Earth years. The Earth's
coordinates were entered and a
wormhole was opened that would lead
directly to Earth.
